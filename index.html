The Vantara

APEX Vantara — The luxury Full-Size Flagship

The APEX Vantara is the definitive expression of what we believe a luxury SUV should be. As our full-size flagship, the Vantara represents decades of engineering refinement, hand-crafted detail, and uncompromising all-terrain capability — combined in a single, unmistakable silhouette. Every Vantara is hand-assembled at our Coachworks facility by master craftsmen who treat each vehicle as a singular work of automotive art.


Where the rest of the Apex range explores different facets of luxury and capability, the Vantara delivers all of it — in full measure. A commanding road presence built on a precision-milled aluminum chassis. An interior trimmed in full-grain Italian leather, open-pore walnut, and hand-polished aluminum. An adaptive air suspension that effortlessly raises the vehicle for serious off-road terrain or settles it for high-speed stability on the highway. And at its heart, a 4.4L twin-turbo V8 producing 530 brake horsepower — silent at idle, ferocious when summoned.
Engineering

Vantara Engineering — Inside the APEX Flagship

4.4L Twin-Turbo V8

The Vantara's hand-built powerplant delivers 530 bhp and 553 lb-ft of torque. Capable of 0-100 km/h in 4.4 seconds despite the Vantara's full-size proportions.

8-Speed ZF Transmission

The Vantara's transmission delivers seamless shifts across all conditions, whether you're crossing sand, climbing rock, or settling into a long highway stretch.

Adaptive Air Suspension

The Vantara raises itself for serious off-road terrain and lowers for high-speed stability — all automatically, all imperceptibly to occupants.
